# Engineering Learnings

Record only reusable lessons that are likely to improve future work. Do not duplicate ordinary commit history.

## What belongs here

A learning is worth recording when it captures:

- a failure mode that could recur;
- a non-obvious framework or provider behavior;
- a deployment or infrastructure lesson;
- a testing technique that prevented a real class of regression;
- an architectural constraint discovered through implementation.

## Format

```text
## YYYY-MM-DD — Short lesson

Context:
What was being changed or investigated.

Observation:
What actually happened.

Learning:
The reusable conclusion.

Future check:
What should be verified next time.
```

Keep entries factual and technical. Link to a decision when a learning causes a durable architectural or operating rule to change.
